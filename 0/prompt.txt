## What shipped

- `src/lib/motion/math.ts` — `docProgress`, `auroraStyle` (pure, no DOM).
- `src/lib/motion/reduced.ts` — `prefersReducedMotion()`, the JS twin of the CSS media queries.
- `src/lib/motion/scroll.ts` — `onScroll(fn) =&gt; unsubscribe`. Event raises a flag, the rAF reads `scrollY`/`innerHeight`/`scrollHeight` once and passes one shared `ScrollState` object to every subscriber. Attaches on the first subscription (scroll + resize, both `passive`), detaches and cancels the pending frame on the last. Returns a no-op under `reduce` without attaching anything. Subscribing schedules a first frame so a new subscriber starts from the real scroll position instead of bootstrapping its own measurement — that is what keeps issues 08/09 from ever needing a listener or a `getBoundingClientRect` of their own.
- `src/styles/app.css` — `@utility container-page / section-y / section-divider`, plus an `@layer components` aurora block.
- `src/layouts/Base.astro` — full head, aurora, `nav` / default / `footer` slots, one bundled script (aurora subscription + language-choice persistence).
- Both pages reduced to `lang` + the scaffold content block.
